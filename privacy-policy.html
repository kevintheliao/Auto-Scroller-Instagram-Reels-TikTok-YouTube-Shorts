Privacy Policy — Auto Scroller — Instagram Reels, TikTok & YouTube Shorts

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Auto Scroller — Instagram Reels, TikTok & YouTube Shorts ("the extension") automatically advances Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, and YouTube Shorts when the current video ends, and provides keyboard shortcuts for on-page controls.

Data Collection

The extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. It makes no network requests and does not communicate with any external server.

Data Storage

The extension uses Chrome's local storage permission to save your preferences (such as whether auto-scroll is enabled) on your own device. This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

  • storage — saves your on/off preference and settings locally.
  • scripting — injects the auto-scroll and control-card logic into supported pages.
  • Host access (instagram.com, tiktok.com, youtube.com) — required so the extension can detect video playback and trigger scrolling on these sites only. No other sites are accessed.

Third Parties

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.
